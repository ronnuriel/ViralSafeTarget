## Citation practice

A ViralSafeTarget analysis should cite:

1. the exact data accessions and versions;
2. every external tool and version used;
3. the scoring model or paper used for on-target/off-target predictions;
4. the release or commit of ViralSafeTarget;
5. experimental sources used to label genes or benchmark targets.
