# Research-use disclaimer

ViralSafeTarget is an early-stage computational research and educational tool.
Its outputs are hypotheses and prioritization aids only.

The software does not establish:

- successful genome editing;
- viral inactivation or eradication;
- safety, specificity, efficacy, or clinical benefit;
- suitability of any sequence or editor for laboratory, animal, or human use.

No wet-lab protocol is provided. Any experimental work requires qualified
scientific supervision and all applicable institutional biosafety, ethics,
clinical and regulatory approvals.
