# Tool novelty claim

## Defensible wording

To our knowledge, we did not identify an existing platform that jointly integrates
multi-strain viral conservation, model-bounded host-genome risk, guide- and gene-level
targetability, source-provenanced biological evidence, bounded protein-disruption
hypotheses, and mechanism-configured exact-target multiplex escape analysis in one
reproducible project workflow.

## What is not novel by itself

Guide enumeration, on-target scoring, sequence alignment, host off-target search,
annotation mapping, protein databases, literature search, indel consequence concepts,
and multiplex design all exist independently. ViralSafeTarget uses or imports mature
tools where appropriate.

## What may be novel

The potential contribution is the auditable integration and explicit separation of
these axes for virus-first decision support, including direct-versus-ortholog evidence
provenance and an exact set-cover sequence barrier for configured panels.

## Validation still required

A systematic capability matrix linked to official sources, a frozen 257-guide benchmark,
and leave-one-component-out analysis are now committed. The executable comparison
contains complete ViralSafeTarget, Cas-OFFinder, and CRISPRitz metrics; CRISPOR,
CHOPCHOP, and GuideScan2 remain export-required and therefore absent from quantitative
rank comparisons.

Committed exports from those three tools, a multi-strain second-virus scientific case
study, external users, and experimental results are still required before making a
stronger comparative, generalization, or predictive claim. A reference-only BK
polyomavirus run now demonstrates software portability, but not biological
generalization.
