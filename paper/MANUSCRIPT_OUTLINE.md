# Manuscript outline

## Working title

ViralSafeTarget: a reproducible virus-first framework for multi-strain CRISPR
target prioritization, virtual coding disruption, and exact-target escape robustness

## Central claim

ViralSafeTarget integrates viral population conservation, model-bounded host matching,
guide- and gene-level targetability, source-provenanced biological evidence, bounded
coding-sequence hypotheses, and mechanism-configured multiplex comparison in one
reproducible workflow. The HSV-2 case study demonstrates the workflow; it does not
establish editing, safety, efficacy, viral inactivation, treatment, or cure.

## Sections

1. Introduction: why guide quality, gene value, and population robustness are distinct.
2. Methods: inputs, candidate discovery, host screen, virtual knockout, escape model,
   evidence review, caching, and source assertions.
3. Results: exhaustive HSV-2 targetability, guide-versus-gene rank divergence, deep
   panel virtual analysis, held-out support, and four configured multiplex strategies.
4. Discussion: decision support, complementary existing tools, uncertainty, and
   validation roadmap.
5. Limitations: computational-only scope and incomplete evidence/population coverage.

## Publication boundary

The current dataset supports a computational methods/case-study manuscript. A stronger
comparative or biological manuscript still requires a systematic tool benchmark,
approved evidence review, a second-virus run, and independent experimental validation.
