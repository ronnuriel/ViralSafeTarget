# Collaboration summary

ViralSafeTarget is an open, reproducible computational framework for prioritizing
viral CRISPR target hypotheses. The completed HSV-2 source screen evaluated 23,108
eligible candidate-coordinate rows against GRCh38.p14 and produced a 257-guide deep
panel. The new publication snapshot adds coding-coordinate hypotheses, observed
population support, single-nucleotide exact-target counterfactuals, and four configured
multiplex comparisons with complete manifests and explicit uncertainty.

We seek independent review from virologists, genome-editing researchers, and
bioinformaticians, plus collaboration with laboratories able to evaluate a small,
mutually selected candidate subset under their own approved protocols. The repository
does not provide a wet-lab protocol, and the results are not claims of safety,
efficacy, viral inactivation, treatment, or cure.
